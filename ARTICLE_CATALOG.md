# Article Catalog — franchisor.id

This catalog plans operator-facing editorial assets for Indonesian franchisors and businesses preparing to franchise. It is a knowledge architecture, not a publication queue or a substitute for legal, accounting, tax, privacy, security, or sector-specialist advice.

Domain boundaries:

- Franchisor.id owns the network-owner, seller, and operator perspective.
- Franchisee.id independently owns the prospective buyer's discovery, comparison, financing, and due-diligence journey.
- `/peluang-usaha/` and brand-detail pages own live commercial listings; `/profil/`, `/dashboard/`, and `/premium/` own application tasks.
- City/province swaps, thin directory filters, synonyms with identical intent, and guaranteed-return claims are excluded.

Catalog statistics: **19 parent topics, 114 briefs, 6 briefs per topic**. Priority counts: **12 P0, 38 P1, 47 P2, 17 P3**.

Priority legend:

- `P0` — first bounded foundation cluster; publish only after its review gate passes.
- `P1` — next operational core with strong reader and network value.
- `P2` — important depth after foundation evidence is available.
- `P3` — specialist, later-stage, or lower-frequency decision support.

Waves: `W1-foundation` is the measured first cluster; `W2-operating-core`, `W3-network-depth`, and `W4-specialist-growth` are sequencing labels, not bulk-publication approval.
